Pizzas

Pepperoni pizza is a classic American variety featuring tangy tomato sauce, melted mozzarella, and slices of pepperoni, a spicy cured pork and beef salami, making it one of the world's most popular pizzas.

Cheese pizza is a popular, classic dish consisting of a crust topped with tomato sauce and a blend of cheeses, most commonly mozzarella, valued for its melt and stretch. The blend often incorporates sharper cheeses like Provolone or Parmesan, or creamy additions like Ricotta, for added flavor depth.

Hawaiian pizza, invented in Canada, is a debated variety featuring tomato sauce, mozzarella, ham/Canadian bacon, and pineapple chunks, whose unique sweet and savory flavor sparks controversy among purists who argue the fruit disrespects Italian culinary heritage.

A vegan pizza is a plant-based pizza made without any animal products, substituting dairy cheese and meat with vegetables, fruits, plant-based meats, and dairy-free cheeses.
