Pizzas

Pepperoni pizza is a classic American pizza variety topped with tomato sauce, cheese (typically mozzarella), and slices of pepperoni, an American-style spicy salami made from cured pork and beef. It is one of the most popular and widely recognized pizzas worldwide.

Cheese pizza is a popular, classic dish consisting of a crust topped with tomato sauce and a blend of cheeses, most commonly mozzarella. This pizza vary by the blend of cheeses, with Mozzarella (fresh or low-moisture) being the classic base for melt and stretch, often mixed with sharper Provolone, nutty Asiago, or salty Parmesan for flavor depth, while other popular additions include creamy Ricotta, tangy Goat Cheese, buttery Gouda, or bold Cheddar.

Hawaiian pizza is a popular, yet debated, pizza variety topped with tomato sauce, mozzarella cheese, ham or Canadian bacon, and pineapple chunks. This unique combination of ingredients provides a distinct sweet and savory flavor profile that many find appealing. Pizza purists argue pineapple breaks culinary rules and disrespects Italian culinary heritage, even though Hawaiian pizza was invented in Canada by a Greek immigrant.

A vegan pizza is a plant-based pizza made without any animal products, including dairy cheese, meat, or eggs in the crust. It typically uses a variety of vegetables, fruits, plant-based meats, and dairy-free cheeses.
