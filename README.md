### Description
The website aims to showcase a common delicacy—pizza. We notice it is food that scholars usually order, whether that may be for a party or just to be enjoyed alone or with friends. Additionally, both creators play certain games called "Good pizza, Great pizza" and "Cooking Mama", gaining inspiration and/or ideas for our project.

The website introduces the visitors to different pizza flavors featured in a mini-game and highlights top player rankings. It also contains details regarding the creators of the website, along with fun facts and the history of pizza. We hope to create an engaging and interactive experience for pizza lovers, casual eaters, and the general audience alike.
### Webpage Breakdown
**Home:** This shows a banner of our website, as well as a summary of what visitors can expect to see/do in the website. 

**Sign-up:** This will welcome the user with a sign-up area where they can enter a name.

**Types of Pizza:** The website will showcase a total of four pizza flavors, namely, Cheese, Pepperoni, Hawaiian, and Vegan pizzas. We can integrate JavaScript to allow user to click/hover a pizza flavor and show the instruction to create the given pizza flavor in the mini-game.

**Mini-Game:** The mini-game will consist of a playing area which contains a dough (left side) and ingredients necessary to make the four given pizzas (right side). Orders will be generated randomly and a 30-second timer is set. Javascript will be used in this webpage mainly to control the generation of the orders and the timer, as well as modifying buttons such as the start button. It will also be used for responsive interactions with the player in dragging or clicking elements concerned with assembling the pizza in the mini-game. Sound effects (using the integrating of JS) may also be used to make the gameplay more immerisve. We can also use JavaScript to customize the cursor type to kitchen gloves to make it engaging and playful.

**Leaderboard:** Players' inputted usernames and scores will be recorded automatically. We can use Javascript to sort and update scores of each player in real time. The webpage will then display/highlight the current top-10 players. We used the Firebase Database to make sure that the leaderboard can be seen in different computers by different users.

**About us:** This page will introduce the creators behind the website. It will also contain the reason why our website was created. 

**Facts:** This will include fun facts and the history of pizza.

**Comments:** This will serve as a platform where users can anonymously give comments or feedback regarding the website.

**Credits:** The sources we used will be linked here.

### FINAL MODIFICATION PROPOSAL
Users will be enabled to delete or update their uploaded comments under the "Comments" webpage. Buttons for deleting or updating will be shown upon clicking the three-dots menu icon located on the upper-right corner of a given comment. CRUD operations (UD) will be based on this website: https://firebase.google.com/docs/database/web/read-and-write, due to the nature of our database (Firebase Database).
